GATE 3

The present perfect

↺

We use it to connect the past to now. Conecta el pasado con el presente.

1 · Life experiences

I’ve traveled by ferry.

It’s an experience I’ve had in my life. We don’t say when.

2 · Something that continues

She’s lived here for five years.

5 years agoNOW

She still lives here now.

GATE 5

Present perfect or simple past?

⇄
Present perfect

I’ve taken a train before.

An experience up to now. No exact time.

Simple past

I took a train last summer.

A finished time in the past.

Compare

“Have you ever taken a train?” “Yes, I took one last summer.”
