Countries and languages · Activity 2

Country, nationality or language?

Three kinds of words help us talk about where we’re from and what we speak.

CountryMexicoI’m from Mexico.
NationalityMexicanI’m Mexican.
LanguageSpanishI speak Spanish.
Your turn: I’m from ___. I’m ___. I speak ___.

Flip the flags

Tap a card to see the nationality and language examples. Press ▶ to hear them.

💡

Careful! Some words are a nationality and a language: I’m Japanese. I speak Japanese. Spanish is spoken in both Mexico and Argentina, and Switzerland has four national languages.
