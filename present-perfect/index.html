For and since

SinceWhen did it start?I’ve lived here since 2023.Monday · 8 a.m. · I was a child

How long…?

Ask about the duration of an activity or situation that continues now.

Pattern

How long + have / has + subject + past participle?

How long have you studied English?

I’ve studied English for two years.

I’ve studied English since 2024.

Either answer tells us how long.
